Cockpit: the note's links in now run down the left side as a dim, curved column of names, centred top to bottom, and its links out run down the right. Each name has a dot in its level colour, and they're listed most relevant first. A busy note shows its top 20 and "+N more". F or R dims the side you've turned off. Click a name to lock on it, and click it again to fly there.
