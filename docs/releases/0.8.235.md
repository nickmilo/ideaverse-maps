# 0.8.235

- **Recent**: the notes changed most lately, newest first, in the upper right of the map, in the HUD's own style (the same gentle arc, a hairline border, a soft veil so the names read over the map). Ten show; the wheel scrolls on to thirty. Each row leads with its emoji (Display → Assign emojis) and ends with its age (3h, 2d, 7d). Click to open it as the primary note (⌘ for a new orb, Shift to link it in); hold ⌘ for a mini preview. Click the header to tuck it away. A note you change moves to the top at once. When Links is on too, it sits just below. Display → Recent turns it off.
- The HUDs now draw over the year labels, so they always read.
