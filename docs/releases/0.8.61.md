## What's new in 0.8.61

- **Arena's Board (experimental),** a second style in Arena's gear, after a shaped-canvas painting. Your slots become framed squares on a board whose outline follows them, with a soft shadow under it.
  - **The squares:** each note is a flat square of colour, bigger for a higher score: yellow for hubs, teal for maps of maps, blue for maps of content. A thin, slightly skewed frame shows its place on the board.
  - **The wires:** links run like a circuit, bending once on the way, in their link's colour: purple for up, yellow for related, blue for links in the text.
  - **Where a wire meets a frame:** a dot marks a linked note, a ring a map, and a ring with a dot a hub. A short thick bar marks a link that runs both ways.
  - **Picking:** click a square to pick its note, and its frame and wires light up. Empty slots show as small dashed squares; click one to fill it.
  - The board keeps its light paper on dark themes too, like a panel on a gallery wall.
