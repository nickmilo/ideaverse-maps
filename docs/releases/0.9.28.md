# 0.9.28

- **A note that opens beside you is the active note.** Click a tab and its note opens in the support note and becomes active right away: the Links HUD lists its links, and in Focus the graph behind regroups around it. Click back into the primary note to return.
- **Holodeck: the inner edges drag.** The grip between the primary and support notes works in the Holodeck: drag either inner edge and one note widens as the other narrows (the support note keeps at least 300px). With only the primary open, its right edge sets its width. The Holodeck remembers its own width, apart from the normal map's; double-click the edge for the default.
