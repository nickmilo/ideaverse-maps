# 0.9.1 · The Deck (first version)

A new view for writing, with no graph behind it. Two commands (Command palette; give them hotkeys in Settings → Hotkeys):

- **Open the Deck.** The note you're on becomes the **primary note**, in the middle, the biggest rectangle. Right beside it, in a straight column, its **Recents** and **Links**. On the right, two **support** slots: the one you click into grows, the other shrinks. Top left, the note that was primary before (**Previous**, read-only; click it to swap back). Below it, a pinned **sidekick** note (a map of content, usually; 📌 on the primary pins it there).
  - Click a Recents or Links row to make it the primary; ⌘-click it to open it in a support slot (the empty one first); or drag a row onto any slot.
  - A link clicked inside any Deck note stays in the Deck the same way.
  - Each support note and the sidekick has a **links drawer** (the panel icon in its header) that slides out over its right side.
  - On a narrower window, the left column steps aside first.
- **Pop out Deck Mini.** The note you're on in its own small window, with its Recents and Links in a drawer you can close: made for taking notes in a meeting beside Zoom. The main map stays where it is.

Each Deck and Deck Mini remembers its own notes, separately from the map and from each other, and comes back after a restart. First version: tell me what feels off.
