## What's new in 0.8.56

- **Contrails,** a new switch in Display. Each jump from one note to the next leaves a subtle vapor trail along a gentle arc: a thin bright line inside a soft cloud that widens, breaks into puffs and fades as it ages. Your last ten jumps stay on the map, the older ones fainter, and they follow the notes as the map moves. It's a breadcrumb trail you can see.
- **Rocket,** a small switch under Contrails (off by default). A tiny rocket flies each new jump, trailing its contrail behind it.
