# 0.8.289

**The Starship gets the HUD.** Its list of links now lives in the same Links panel as on every other map, under Recents and on the same arc:

- It lists the links of the note you're at: up links first, then by relevance, ← in, → out, ↔ both.
- Click a row to lock onto that note's panel, and click it again to fly there. The locked note's row stays lit.
- Hovering a row shows its preview and runs a tether to its panel. Shift-click or ⌘-click opens it in a new orb or as the support note.
- The F and R scopes dim the other side's links. Star names keep clear of the panel, and the panel stops above the scopes and overview map along the bottom.
- Tuck it away to its Links tab like anywhere else. Phones keep the old list drawn on the view.
