Link by dragging (Nick: "option drag… pulling the link of a note from the graph"). Hold ⌥ (Alt) and drag any note on the map:

- **Into an editor** (the left editor, the support note, any Obsidian tab): its [[link]] lands right where you let go.
- **Onto another note's dot:** the link goes into that note's **related** property. A label above the dot says where it's going ("→ related of Pace Layers").
- **Hold Control** (at the start, or before you let go) and it goes into **up** instead.

The note you drop onto is changed without opening it, and the new link draws itself on the map. A link that's already there isn't added twice. Uses your property names from Settings. Off in the Cockpit, Arena and Kandinsky, which use ⌥ for their own things.
