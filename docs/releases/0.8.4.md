## What's new in 0.8.4

- **Starfield: Galaxy glow → Clear.** A new first choice beside Soft, Normal and Bright, for when the galaxy light gets in the way of seeing your notes. Galaxies become faint tinted regions with no light of their own, the faraway background stars fade back, and every note is a crisp dot: maps and hubs with a thin dark rim and a ring around Levels 4 and 5. The only stars you see are your notes. It works in 2D, 3D and Star Chart.
