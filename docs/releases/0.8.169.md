Cockpit in Links: autopilot arrivals flow the same way Starfield's do now. Arriving used to snap the ship back home and fly the new links in with a warp flash. Now the ship keeps its speed and glides on to a stop, keeping its heading, and the new note's links fade in ahead of it. Once it stops, the view quietly becomes the usual one; nothing on screen moves. Touching the keys, dragging or a new autopilot leg ends the glide where you are. Clicked jumps still use the warp.
