## What's new in 0.7.45

- **Saved is a telescope now** (it was a star).
- **Make your own groups of views.** Press **+ Group** at the top of Saved and name it, say Annual Review or Daily Drivers.
  - Groups fold open and shut, like Filter's lists.
  - Move a view into a group from the small menu on its row.
  - Views in no group sit at the end, under "No group".
  - Double-click a group's name to rename it. × deletes a group; its views stay, with no group.
  - Groups live in your saved-views file, with your views.
- **↓ as well as ↑.** Views move up and down within their group, and groups move up and down among themselves.
- A row's buttons show when you point at it (always on phones), so the names get the room. Saved is a little wider too.
