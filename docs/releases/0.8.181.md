Linear Calendar: **Last 30 days**, a new shape. It always ends today, with big day boxes so a busy day has room. A **Days as** row picks how the 30 days are laid out:

- **Weeks:** Monday to Sunday rows, the classic calendar month (the default).
- **Two rows:** 15 days each.
- **One line:** all 30 in a row.

Weekends are shaded and today is outlined. The Names setting (Natural, More, Many) works here too, and saved views remember the layout.
