# 0.8.350

- **Starship retired as its own map.** It's gone from the maps menu (and from a custom order that had it); Starfield is the Starship now. A saved view or last-used map that pointed at it opens Starfield, with the Ship | Chart switch. The old one-note's-links Starship is gone with it.
- **Display: "Show collection links (coral)"**, with a clearer "?": on, lines from the picked note to each of its collections (Movies, People, Things…) in coral, on the maps and in the Starship, drawn only, never counted; off, none. Other property links (actors, director, by…) always show, in their own colors.
