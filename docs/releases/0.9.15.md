# 0.9.15

- **Fixed dates in Filter → Years.** A new **Dates** row under Recent: pick a from and a to day (both included), and the map shows that span and nothing moves it, however much time passes. Recent fades while fixed dates are on; Clear goes back.
- **"Keep these dates"** turns the Recent window you're looking at (say, Last 2 weeks) into those exact dates, in one click.
- Saved views remember fixed dates, so a view saved this way, and a saved view embedded in a note, always shows the same span. Works by the Date property and with "Organize by most recently modified" alike.
