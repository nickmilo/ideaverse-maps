Cockpit in Starfield gets Star Chart, when it's on in Starfield's ⚙. Each layer follows its own switch there:

- **Rings:** each Level 5 hub ahead wears a ring with a dial of twelve ticks. The cockpit note's ring is larger and brighter.
- **Turning orbits:** a dashed orbit turns round each ring, with a moon.
- **Readouts:** a card beside the cockpit note gives level and score, links in and out, the year its linked notes began, and how many came this year.
- **Lines between hubs:** links between Level 4 and 5 notes, stepped back as in Starfield while a note is picked. Hubs right beside the ship are left out so a line doesn't slash across the view.
