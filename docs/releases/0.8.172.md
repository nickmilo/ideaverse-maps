Linear Calendar: two new shapes beside Whole and Half months.

- **Life in weeks:** a row per year and a box per week, with your notes filling the weeks. Type a birth year under Born and it becomes the classic poster: the 90 years from your birth, ages down the side (0 · 1981, 5 · 1986…), a gap every decade, and the weeks you've lived shaded. With no birth year, it shows the years you have notes.
- **Years stacked:** a row per year, January to December on one line, so the same season lines up across years. Feb 29 keeps its own column, so every March lines up. It shows the Years range, or every year when the range is a single year.

Both skip stray early years (a few notes dated decades back no longer add rows of empty boxes). Saved views remember the shape and the birth year.
