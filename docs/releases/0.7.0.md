## What's new in 0.7.0

### Bases on every map
- **Filter → Bases** lists every base in your vault, A to Z. Open one and check a view to narrow any map to that view's notes.
- The map reads each base's filters itself, and it matched Obsidian's own results on every test base. Views that use `this` follow the note you pick on the map. If a filter uses something the map can't read yet, that view is marked "can't read" instead of showing the wrong notes.

### A cleaner toolbar
- The **☰** lists every map.
- **Filter** is a funnel right after the ☰, and its Folders, Collections and Bases lists each fold away.
- Sync and Focus sit right after the map tabs.
- On a narrow pane the toolbar stays on one row for as long as it can: Find shrinks to a magnifying glass, then tabs fold into the ☰.
- **Saved** is just its star. **Display** has a new Show area and projects selector switch.
- The **theme orb** shows the theme you're on and opens the list of themes.

### Calmer clicks and names
- A click never zooms or flies away. The note just drifts a little toward the middle.
- The map stays centered when you resize the pane.
- Maps and hubs get named first, crowded names tilt slightly instead of colliding, and no lines are drawn to names.

### Linear Calendar
- It now sits right after Base Builder in the ☰ list.
- It has a Years slider, and Half months for narrow panes.
- Dots on the same day sit at different heights, so their names don't collide.

### Capture
- Square and Vertical shapes for Instagram and TikTok, 30 or 60 fps, and one-click **Share my ideaverse**.

### Fixes
- The scan cache moved out of the vault and into the device's own storage. It could grow past 5 MB on a big vault, which sync services skip, and that could stop the plugin from updating on phones.
- Open note from a base now shows the note beside the base.
- Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) opens a note in a new tab in the left sidebar.
