# 0.9.0 · Starfield

Maps of the Ideaverse 0.9 is the Starfield release. Your vault becomes a night sky you can chart from above or fly through, with time, links and the way home all drawn in the stars. (Editing inside the maps keeps growing in the background; its story is for 1.0.)

## Starfield: Ship and Chart

- **Starfield opens in the Ship.** Pick Starfield (key 3) and you're in the Starship, first-person among your notes. **C** swaps to the **Star Chart**, the flat map of galaxies from above, and back; a small Ship | Chart switch sits at the top right. Star Chart is also its own map in the menu.
- **Galaxies are your hubs.** Their notes swirl round them in disks, with folder gas, galaxy names, hub rings, twinkle, turning galaxies and star haze, each a switch in the ⚙ menu (HUD artifacts, Cosmotic elements). Where a star sits comes from your links and levels alone; arranging Star Chart by Time or by ACE doesn't move it.
- **Fly by hand or let it fly.** R W forward and back, X V pan, S F and E D orbit round a note, T for a slow impulse cruise, two fingers or a drag to steer, Space for autopilot to the note you've locked on, B to fly back. Distances read in light-years (the whole Ideaverse is 100 ly across).

## Links you can see and follow

- **Links HUD.** The note you're at lists its links on the right, written links first, then its collections and every other property in its own folding group (the note's own properties above the ones pointing back). Click a link and the ship **orbits round the note until that link swings into view**, rising about 30° above the note's disk of links; click again to fly there.
- **Link colors that mean something.** Up gold, related purple, above blue, below green, collections coral (or slate, your choice), other properties slate; a dark casing keeps each color true over bright nebulae. **Color all links by Idea Emergence** carries the same colors into your notes, the backlinks pane and the Starfield theme, and a dashed outline marks only links with no note behind them.
- **Show all links** draws every link among the stars ahead, faintly. **Show the way home** draws the fewest links back to Home (or a note you choose) through the stars, each stop named.

## Animations

- **Orbit, Dynamic.** G circles the note you're at, mostly left or right, now and then climbing or diving, never quite level; Calm, Slow or Lively. **Ideaverse Orbit** pulls back to circle the whole sky.
- **Wander.** Links (from note to linked note), Random (hyperspace jumps across the sky) or Wonder (a slow cruise). With Orbit on too, the ship circles each note while it lingers, and a jump or a flight never breaks the orbit.
- **Timelapse.** Watch your Ideaverse grow: only the notes made by each month show, each new star arriving on a short glowing streak (Comets) or a swirl (Swirl in, the default here, or Swirl out) before it blooms, galaxies brightening as their notes arrive. Ship ⇄ Chart keeps it playing; the date floats in the sky and fades a few seconds after it stops.
- Everything lives in one **Animations** dock, with a "?" on each part, and the Starship menu's **DYNAMIC: Wander · Orbit · More** row.

## The HUD

- **Recents and Links** ride one curve down the map's right edge, each tucking to its own tab, with emojis, ages, relevance scores and colored names. **HUD opacity** goes down to 0% (the default), and **See-through tabs** lets every tucked tab follow it.

## Everywhere else

- **The maps menu:** Emergence, Ideaverse, Starfield and Calendar up top (keys 1–4); Sensemakers, Star Chart and the rest below; Experimental's Unfinished and Misc. **Ideaverse | Obsidian** get their own switch and C too.
- **Display**, regrouped under mini headers (Link colors, Heads Up Display, Editors, Interface, Wayfinding, Sidebar Selection and Customization), with a "?" on each switch.
- **Base Builder:** Scatter always works (Link score is an axis), and **Timeline of** lets you pick any property with years or dates.
- **Close-Up (Z)** on the Ideaverse, Skyline and more; **Focus** blooms in place; the trail tucks away; a guard asks by name before deleting a note open in the map.
- Releases now publish only once every file is attached, so an update can't arrive without its styles.

Retired: the standalone Starship map (Starfield is the Starship now), Posters and People (Base Builder covers them), and the dev layout.
