Cockpit ⚙: Rear-view mirror, a new switch (off by default) that puts a mirror where the note card sits. It shows what lies behind the ship, flipped left to right as a mirror is: galaxies as soft glows, maps and hubs as stars, and the cockpit note's links as dots in their link colours, the nearest six named. The cockpit note shows as a yellow ring when it's behind you.
