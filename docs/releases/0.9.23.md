# 0.9.23

- **The Links HUD follows the note you're in.** Click into the support note and the HUD shows its links; click back into the primary note and it shows the primary's. It works the same in and out of the Holodeck.
- **Drag a tab onto a note.** Drag an orb tab out of its row or column and drop it on the primary note or the support note, and that note changes to the dragged one. The note under the pointer gets a dashed outline. Dropped on the primary note, it overrides a pin, and the primary note stays pinned.
- **Editor opacity goes down to 15%,** in Display and on the Holodeck's slider, for a quick look at the map behind the notes.
