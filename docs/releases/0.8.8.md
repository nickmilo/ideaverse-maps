## What's new in 0.8.8

- **Focus in Starfield frames the note and its hub.** Focusing a note in Starfield (2D and Star Chart) centres it and zooms so its galaxy's hub sits just inside the edge of the view, so you always see where the note belongs. Focusing a hub itself shows its whole galaxy. Moving the focus to a linked note frames that one the same way, and the overview map shows where you landed.
