Cockpit on a phone: look around by moving it (Nick). A new switch in the Cockpit's ⚙, Look around by moving the phone (phones only, off by default): turn the phone to turn the ship, tilt it to look up and down, upright or on its side.

The motion adds to your finger, so dragging still steers. It pauses while autopilot, Wander, the game or an editor is in charge. On iPhone, turning the switch on asks for motion access once.
