## What's new in 0.8.38

- **A thin strip under each editor's name.** It shows the note's level, its score and its links (for example "21 in, 6 out"), then small buttons for what sits above and below it: "↑ 1 above" and "↓ 21 below". Pills for its up and related links follow, and appear only when the note has them. Click any of these to open its list in place under the strip. Click a note in the list to go there: Shift-click opens it in a new tab and ⌘-click opens it as the support note. The strip keeps up as you add links.
- **The note panel steps aside in Edit mode.** When an editor opens, the note panel tucks into its tab on the right edge, since the editor and its strip already show the note. Click the tab to bring the panel back. It returns on its own when the editor closes.
