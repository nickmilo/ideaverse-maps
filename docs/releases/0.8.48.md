## What's new in 0.8.48

- **Previews: Off, Fewer, Natural or Max,** in Display → Edit mode, in place of the slider.
- **One limiter for both maps.** Starfield now keeps its previews in check the way Compass does: a preview only shows where it covers no other preview, the note you're on and its name, the orbs, the editors, the overview map or the zoom buttons. The level sets how many may show and, in Starfield, how big a galaxy must be on screen to get one, so zooming in brings more. Natural is the default.
