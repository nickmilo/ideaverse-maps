# 0.8.327

- **Starship Orbit: Calm, Slow and Lively.** Dynamic orbit now comes at the three speeds the other maps use, chosen in the Animations dock (G still starts and stops it).
- **Never dead level.** Dynamic orbit always climbs or dives at least a degree; its gentle stretches tilt 1–6°, its bolder ones about 30°.
- **Better resting spots when a Links HUD pick swings into view.** The ship tries the whole circle round the note and stops where the pick sits about a third of the way out from the center note, near its height, for the least swing — instead of tucking it close beside the center note. A note already in view moves to that spot too.
