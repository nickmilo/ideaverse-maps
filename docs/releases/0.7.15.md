## What's new in 0.7.15

- **One maps button.** The ☰ and the current map's name are now a single button at the start of the toolbar, and the four map tabs are gone. Click it to open the maps menu.
- **The maps menu** lists every map with a small icon before its name: the four main maps first, then the rest, then Experimental, with thin lines between the groups.
- **This map's settings sit beside it.** The current map's variants and options open to the right of the menu, level with the map's name. On a narrow pane or a phone they open just below it instead.
- **Filter** now sits right after the maps button.
- **Keys 1–4** jump to Emergence, Ideaverse, Starfield and Sensemakers.
