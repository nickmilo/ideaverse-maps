## What's new in 0.8.62

- **Kandinsky, a new map** (in the maps menu, after Arena), after his 1913 colour study "Squares with Concentric Circles".
  - **The tiles:** each note is a square tile of concentric, hand-drawn circles. The tile's colour comes from its top folder.
  - **The circles:** a tile holds one circle more than the note's level, so an unlinked note has two and a hub six. The circles are bigger for a higher score, and the innermost one is the level's colour.
  - **The gear:** show your top notes by rank (read left to right, then down) or the note you picked with its links, as 24, 48, 96 or 192 tiles.
