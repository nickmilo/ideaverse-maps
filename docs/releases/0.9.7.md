# 0.9.7 · Deck

- **The full note strip in the Deck,** as in the map's editors: L# · up · related │ in · out │ #rank · folder. Click up, related, in or out to open that list under the strip; click a note in it to open it (⌘-click: a support slot, or drag it onto a slot).
- **Deck Mini's drawer resizes:** drag its left edge wider for long names, or narrower for more writing room (140–460 px). Only its width changes while you drag.
