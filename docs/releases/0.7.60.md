## What's new in 0.7.60

- **Show emojis on more maps.** Gravity, Starfield, Obsidian, Ideaverse, Chord and Tesla (one switch for both), Spiral, Lifeline and Tree now have a **Show emojis** switch in their gear, as Linear Calendar, Emergence and Sensemakers do. Each map remembers its own switch, and saved views keep them. Maps where notes are boxes, tiles, books or cards (Treemap, Mosaic, Bookshelf, Skyline, Base Builder, Posters) keep their look, and so does Corona, where emojis got lost on the sphere.
