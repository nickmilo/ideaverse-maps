## What's new in 0.8.40

- **The strip under an editor's name reads in a new order:** level, up, related, above, below, then the score.
- **The score is a pill.** It shows just the number. Click it to open the score's details in place: links in ("linked from"), links out ("links to"), the notes that link up to it, and the score itself, with a line on why the note has its level and where its score ranks.
- The count of links in and out moved from the strip into the score's details.
