Arena → Stonehenge now shows the monument as it stands today, not the complete ancient one:

- **Sarsen circle:** 17 sarsens stand and 8 lie where they fell. Six lintels are still up (130, 101, 102, 105, 107 and 122), and two fallen lintels lie on the grass.
- **Trilithons:** three still stand whole. The Great Trilithon is down to stone 56, with 55 and its lintel lying broken over the Altar Stone. The north trilithon is down to stone 60.
- **Bluestones:** the ones that show above the turf in the circle, and 14 of the horseshoe's 19.
- **Outer stones:** the Heel Stone, the Slaughter Stone lying flat, and two Station Stones (93 standing, 91 fallen).

Fallen stones can hold notes too. Notes placed on the stones in 0.8.82 stay where they were, unless the stone is gone today.
