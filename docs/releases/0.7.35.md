## What's new in 0.7.35

- **At most 30 changed notes a day** when Filter → Years → Organize by most recently modified is on. One edit to a map can change every note that links to it, so a day can show 50 or 1,000 "modified" notes that are really one change.
  - Notes changed within two minutes of each other, eight or more in a row, count as a burst and keep only their biggest note (highest level, then most links).
  - Then each day keeps its 30 most recent.
  - On Emergence and Space-Time, a small "+N more" under each day says how many notes it let go.
