## What's new in 0.8.57

- **Compass shows more previews, the most related first.**
  - Previews go to the notes most related to the centre, whether Relevance is on or off.
  - Up to 18 at Max, smaller the more there are.
  - Each preview can sit above, below or beside its note. The six most related can also sit further out, joined to their note by a thin dotted line, so a crowded middle can't push them out.
