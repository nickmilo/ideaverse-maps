## What's new in 0.7.2

- **Dark is retired.** LYT Mode is the dark theme now. If you were on Dark, you move to LYT Mode, and a dark Obsidian gets LYT Mode until you pick a theme.
- The **Match Obsidian** circle is white and purple, so it reads at a glance in the theme list.
- In **Filter**, click anywhere on the Folders, Collections or Bases header to fold that list; the arrow now sits after the name.
- Fixed: at certain widths the toolbar could flicker between one row and two, several times a second.
- The README pictures were retaken with the current toolbar.
