Cockpit HUD, a bigger view:

- **No console bar:** with the Cockpit frame off (the default), the view runs to the bottom edge. The two scopes and a smaller, see-through note card float over it. Turning the frame on brings back the full console and struts.
- **Up links at the top:** the note's up links sit at the top of the HUD, just under the orbs, as ▲ names in the up colour. Click one to lock on, and again to fly there.
- **Scrolling the names:** the names down the sides scroll. Use the wheel over a list, or click its last line ("+N more ↓"). The header shows which ones you're seeing ("1–20 of 73").
- **With the left editor open:** links in no longer hide behind it. Every link goes down the right as one list, each note once, marked ← in, → out, ↔ both, most relevant first. The left scope and the note card move clear of the editor.
