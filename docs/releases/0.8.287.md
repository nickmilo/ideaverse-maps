# 0.8.287

The HUD, take three:

- **Recents and Links are two panels on one arc.** They're separate again, but they share the same curve down the right edge, the same width and the same right edge, so they read as one HUD.
- **Links has its own slot, a little lower on the right.** It sits just under where Recents ends (at 7 notes). Tuck Recents away and Links stays exactly where it was.
- **Recents sits a little lower when Links shows**, out of the top corner. On its own it stays near the top.
- **The outline fades with Opacity.** At 0% it's gone. Only the dividers under the Recents and Links headers stay at any opacity.
- **Scrolling a long list (50 Recents) is steady.** The panel's shape no longer changes as you scroll. Rows slide under the divider instead of into the header, and a row at the top or bottom edge fades out instead of being cut off.
