# 0.9.146: Runs and landmarks

When notes are placed by coordinates:

- **Runs.** Fly a route stop by stop. In the Ship, the **Runs** button sits beside Galaxy. In Star Chart, use **Fly a run…** in the Starfield menu. The route's name and its first and last stops stay at the top of the field, along with your place in the run. The lane is drawn in gold, and each stop in the Ship is a hyperspace jump. Selecting any other note ends the run.
- **Landmarks.** Notes with `landmark: 1` always keep their names on the Chart and in the Ship's overview.
