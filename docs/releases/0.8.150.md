Cockpit, flying by hand (Nick: "speeds by too fast"; "slow down a bit sooner when the gas is let off"). In Starfield, holding W builds speed more gently (900 a second, was 1,600) to a lower top speed (1,800, was 3,200). Near galaxies it eases down to 500. Let go and the ship slows about twice as fast as before, in both worlds. Autopilot is unchanged.
