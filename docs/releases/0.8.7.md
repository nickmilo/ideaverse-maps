## What's new in 0.8.7

- **Starfield's overview map.** A small picture of the whole sky sits in the bottom right, beside the zoom buttons, with a box showing the part you're looking at and a dot for the note you're on. In Focus, the focused notes show as small dots, so when a jump takes you far across the sky you can see where you landed. Click or drag in it to move the view there. Turn it off with **Show the overview map** in Starfield's gear.
- **Display → Starfield link colors.** A switch that gives every map Starfield's original link colors: gold for links above, blue for links below, lilac for related, pale gold for up, slate for other properties. The note panel and the legend follow along, so the colors always agree. Off, every map uses the theme's colors.
- **Fixed: a signpost in Focus.** In Star Chart, clicking a signpost while in Focus now moves the focus to that note, with all of its links, as clicking a star does. It used to select it and show only the link you came from.
- **Star Chart's readout** counts "new this year" by the real calendar year.
