# 0.9.2 · Deck

- **Back and forward** on the primary note, both support notes and the sidekick, each with its own history.
- **A wider sidekick:** the left column starts at 340 px, and you can drag its right edge to make it wider or narrower (240–560 px); the Deck remembers it.
- **Quieter headers:** a note's header just says what it is (Primary, Support, Sidekick); the note's own title is right below it. Previous, which has no editor, still names its note.
- Drawers on the support notes and the sidekick show only that note's links (Recents stay with the primary).
