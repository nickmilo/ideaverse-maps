# 0.8.322

- **Orbit works in the Starship.** Press Orbit's ▶ in the Animations dock and the ship circles the note you're at, eyes on it. Two choices replace Calm, Slow and Lively there:
  - **Lateral:** a level circle, left to right.
  - **Dynamic:** mostly left or right, but now and then the circle tilts about 30°, so the ship climbs or dives as it goes. It turns back toward level before it gets too high or low, and sometimes changes direction.
- Any key, drag or Wander takes over from Orbit while you use it.
