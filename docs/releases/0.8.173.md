Two fixes:

- **Cockpit Wander, Drift:** no more loops (Works → Works in Progress → Works…). On arrival the Cockpit locks onto the most relevant link, which here is the note you just came from. Drift was meant to pick a new link every few seconds while lingering. But its counter kept growing across arrivals, so after a few stops it never picked again and flew back along the auto-lock. The counter now restarts at each note, and Wander never heads to a note it has already visited while a new one is in reach.
- **Linear Calendar, Life in weeks:** the Years range now filters the rows, as it does for Years stacked. Set the range to a single year and you get the whole picture again: the 90 years from your birth year, or every year with notes. Ages and decade gaps follow your birth year, whatever range you show.
