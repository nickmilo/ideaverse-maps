Cockpit's two scopes now work as in X-Wing (1993). The left scope shows every panel ahead of the ship, and the right one every panel behind it, so you keep a sense of where the notes are once you've flown past them.

A dot's distance from the middle is how far it lies off the nose (or the tail): the middle is dead ahead (or dead behind), and the rim is square to your side. The locked note is the ringed yellow dot. Links in and out are still listed by name down the sides, and F and R still filter them.
