New experimental map: Cockpit (maps menu → Experimental → Unfinished).

The note you pick is the cockpit, and its links hang ahead of you as panels: links in on the left, links out on the right, links both ways straight ahead. What it links up to sits above the horizon, and what sits below it is beneath. The strongest links are nearest. Fly into a panel and that note becomes the cockpit.

- Fly: W or ↑ forward, S or ↓ back, A D or ← → to turn, Q E to look up and down. You can also drag to steer and scroll to fly.
- Click a panel to lock on. Click it again, double-click it or press Space to fly there on autopilot. Tab moves the lock, and B flies back.
- F shows only the links in, R only the links out. The two scopes count them.
- The message line names the closest relative the note doesn't yet link up or related to, and the lock starts on it.
- The centre display shows the level, rank, score and links, and what the next level takes. Two dials show links made this year and the note's age. Eject unpins the editor.
