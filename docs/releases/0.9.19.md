# 0.9.19

- **Holodeck.** A new button in the primary note's header turns it on. The notes come forward and the map stays behind them: dimmed, still updating, out of reach. The orb tabs become a column of tabs on the far left, each with its orb and its name; a long name wraps to a second line, and only one that still doesn't fit gets smaller. Click a tab and its note opens just to the right of the primary note; click that tab again and it becomes the primary note. Drag tabs up and down to reorder them. The same button turns it off.
