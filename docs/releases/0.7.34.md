## What's new in 0.7.34

- **Zoom into recent work.** With Filter → Years → **Organize by most recently modified** on, the years slider gives way to **Last Day · Week · Month · Year · All**, counted back from right now. Pick one and the map keeps just the notes changed in that window.
- On Emergence (by time) and Space-Time, the timeline stretches across the window you pick:
  - **Day** is marked every 3 hours.
  - **Week** is marked by day (Tue 20, Wed 21…).
  - **Month** is marked by week.
  - **Year** is marked by month.
- Other maps simply show the notes from the window.
- Modified times now keep the hour and minute, so a single day spreads out.
- Notes from before the window stay off the map unless **Show notes outside the date range** is on; then they gather in an "Earlier" strip.
- With the switch off, Years works exactly as before.
