# 0.9.140

- **Place by coordinates gets regions.** With Starfield placed by coordinates, notes are grouped by folder (at the first folder depth that gives 3 to 40 groups) and each group glows in its own tint with its name and count, in the Chart and the Ship. A note without coordinates, like a region's own note, now sits where its linked notes gather most thickly instead of at their average, so a ring-shaped region's note no longer lands in the middle of a different one.
