Cockpit Wander Mode is calmer in all three modes, and every hyperspace trip plays out in full.

- **Lingering:** about 9 seconds with each note at the default 6 s pace (was 3), drifting gently.
- **Turning:** turning toward the next note takes its time, up to 5 seconds instead of about 1.
- **Jumps** (and long trips in Links) are real flights: the ship turns, then flies the whole way at full speed with the streaks, instead of cutting to the next note after a second.
- **Short hops** between nearby notes glide at a calmer speed.

A new note comes about every 15 to 20 seconds instead of every few. Wander Mode's seconds slider still sets the pace.
