## What's new in 0.7.50

**Saved (the telescope) is calmer:**

- **Drag and drop.** Drag a view onto a group's name to move it in, or between two views to put it there, joining their group. Drag onto "No group" to take it out. Drag a group's name to reorder the groups. A thin line or a highlight shows where it will land.
- **Less on each row.** The ↑ ↓ arrows and the group menu are gone on a computer, since dragging does both. Pointing at a view shows only Update and ×, so its name stays readable. On a phone, where dragging isn't available, the arrows and group menu stay.
- **Views sit further in under their group**, so the group and its views are easy to tell apart.
- **No hover hints** pop up over the list.
- **The footer** says "Saved in" in small type, then the folder as a small pill (click it to move the file). Before you pick a folder, the pill says Choose folder.
