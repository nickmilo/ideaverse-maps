## What's new in 0.8.44

- **Arena has a ring button.** A new button sits in the zoom column while you're in Arena: a dashed ring with a dot. Pick a note, and it sits at the centre. Click the button, and the note slides out from the centre into the next empty slot on the ring. If the ring is full, it adds a slot, up to 20.
- Arena's gear, tidied: the Slots slider and its buttons now match the gear's style.
