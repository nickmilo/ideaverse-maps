# 0.9.5 · Deck

- **⌘ preview, drag to a slot.** Hold ⌘ over a Recents or Links row (or an orb) and its preview appears beside it. Drag the preview onto the primary note, a support slot or the sidekick to open it there; click it to make it the primary (⌘-click: a support slot).
- **Orb tabs in the Deck.** The map's own orb tabs sit above the Deck, the same list: click one for the primary, ⌘-click for a support slot, or drag it onto any slot.
- **Narrow windows:** the left column folds into two edge tabs (Previous, Sidekick); click either to open the column over the primary note, again to close it.
