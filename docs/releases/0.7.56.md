## What's new in 0.7.56

- **Emergence can show emojis.** Emergence's gear menu now has a **Show emojis** switch, like Linear Calendar's. Turn it on and each note's emoji takes the place of its dot on Main and Space-Time, bigger the higher its level. Notes without an emoji keep their dot, and Planetarium keeps its dots. The emojis come from the same place as Linear Calendar's: a column of one of your bases, set in **Settings → Emoji labels**.
