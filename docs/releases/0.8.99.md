Cockpit in Starfield: signposts. Round the cockpit note's ring, a marker points toward each Level 4 or 5 hub it links to, wherever that hub is, even behind you (marked ↺). Each is named in the HUD's capitals, and Level 5 hubs are gold. Click a signpost to lock on, and click again to fly there. Its own switch is in the Cockpit's ⚙.
