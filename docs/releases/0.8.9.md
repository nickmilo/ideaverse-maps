## What's new in 0.8.9

- **A tighter Starfield menu.** 2D, 3D and Star Chart are one row of three, with a line about the one you're on beneath it. Star Chart's extras sit under it, two to a row, and Twinkle, Turning galaxies and the overview map are plain switches in one list.
- **Star Chart's extras, one switch each:** Lines between hubs, Readouts, Folder gas and Turning orbits, so you can mix them. Anyone who had the single switch on keeps all four on. Saved views remember each one.
- **The overview map starts off.** Turn it on with **Show the overview map** in Starfield's menu.
- **Scoring → Balance levels by vault size.** A switch for smaller ideaverses: Level 4 starts at the top 2% of linked notes by score and Level 3 at the top 5%, so a vault of a few hundred notes gets all five levels of idea emergence too. Recommended for ideaverses under 2,000 notes; the panel says whether yours is one. Off, the cut-offs are the ones you set.
