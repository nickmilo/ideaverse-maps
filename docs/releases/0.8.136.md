Cockpit: the mini previews are editable.

- **Click a preview** and it becomes a live Obsidian editor in the same spot, a little larger, with Live Preview, properties and links. Typing saves to the note, and the map follows your links as you write.
- **Finish** with Esc, the ×, or a click anywhere on the view. The previews come back showing what you wrote.
- **Shift-click a preview** to open the note as the support note on the right instead.

Only one preview is editable at a time. A preview holds still while the pointer is on it, so you can reach it from a hovered name.
