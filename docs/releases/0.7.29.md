## What's new in 0.7.29

Letter keys for the map:

- **O** starts and stops Orbit.
- **J**, **K** and **L** pick Orbit's three speeds and start it: Earth, Mercury and LYT Speed on a globe, or Calm, Slow and Lively on a flat map.
- **A** shows or tucks away the Animations.
- **W** starts and stops Wander Mode.
- **T** starts and stops the Timelapse.
- **S** turns Sync with the editor on or off.
- **F** opens Filter.
- **N** turns Focus on or off.

They do nothing while you're typing in a box.
