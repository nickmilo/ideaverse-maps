HUD links (Nick):

- **Scrolling:** the list scrolls with the wheel over it, or with a click on its last line ("+N more ↓"). The header shows which names you're seeing.
- **Preview on hover:** a new switch under Display → HUD links, off by default. Hovering a name shows a small preview of that note beside the list.
- **Tucking away:** the list tucks to the right edge, like the legend. Click its header ("LINKS · 86 ›") and a tab takes its place on the map's right edge, beside the note panel. Click the tab to bring the list back. This also works in the Cockpit, and the choice is shared.
