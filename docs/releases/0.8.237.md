# 0.8.237

- **Recents** (renamed from Recent), rethought: Links is back exactly as it was. Recents lives behind its own tab on the map's right edge, above the Links tab. It opens a small panel with a hairline border and its own scroll: thirty notes, newest first, each with its emoji, its name and its age. Click a row to open it as the primary note (⌘ a new orb, Shift links it in); the panel stays open. › tucks it away.
- Recents has its own **Emojis** mini switch (on by default). It loads your emojis itself, so they show without turning on Show emojis for the map.
