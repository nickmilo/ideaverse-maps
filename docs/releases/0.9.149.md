# 0.9.149: Book cover shapes

A new switch in the Display menu, **Book cover shapes**, draws the Idea Emergence marks as on the Linking Your Thinking book cover:

- Level 5 is a square, yellow to pink.
- Level 4 is an upright triangle, blue to violet.
- Level 3 is a circle, blue to green.

The map, the legend, the trail, the orb tabs, the HUDs and the Links and Recents panes all follow. With the switch off, Level 4 stays a diamond and Level 5 a rounded square, in the theme's colors.
