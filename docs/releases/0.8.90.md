Cockpit → ⚙ → World: Starfield (first step toward flying the cockpit through Starfield).

The ship flies through Starfield itself. Every note sits where 3D Starfield puts it, in its Starfield colour. The galaxies glow in their own colours, disks tilted as in 3D Starfield and nebulae as round clouds, and their names float above them. Maps and hubs shine.

The cockpit note's links are panels where those notes really are, joined to it by faint threads. Fly into one (or lock on and press Space) and you cross Starfield to it. On arrival the ship turns to face the new note's links. Pick a note far away by search and the ship jumps to just above its galaxy, looking down at it.

Speeds are higher here, since the distances are real. "One note's links" is still the default world.
