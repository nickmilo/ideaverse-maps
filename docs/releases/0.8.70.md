## What's new in 0.8.70

- **Kandinsky comes alive.** Drag a tile or shape and let go, and it springs back to its place, overshooting a little. Its linked notes lean after it while you drag.
- **Change Kandinsky's centre with a double-click.** Double-click any shape or tile and it becomes the centre: the great circle in Composition, or the first tile in Squares' Picked + links. A single click still opens the note in the editor.
