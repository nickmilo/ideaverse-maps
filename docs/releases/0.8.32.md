## What's new in 0.8.32

- **Compass, a new map.** One note sits at the centre: the note in the editor, or the one you picked.
  - What it links up to sits above it, and the notes that link up to it sit below. Otherwise higher levels go above and lower levels below.
  - Time runs left to right, earlier to later, stretched so that last week and ten years ago both have room.
  - Each note is joined to the centre in its link color.
- **Relevance (in Compass's gear)** pulls the notes most related to the centre toward it, like radar. Each note keeps its direction, and rings mark the distance. Relevance follows links out from the centre and keeps coming back, counting up and related links double. It also counts notes that share many links with the centre, even ones that don't link to it, and it tempers big hubs so they don't win just for being big.
- **Built for Edit mode.** The note in the main editor is the centre, and Compass frames itself between the editors. Pin the editor to keep the centre in place: notes you pick open as the support note and the map stays put. Unpin it, and picking a note makes that note the new centre. In Edit mode, the closest notes get small previews.
- Find Compass in the maps menu, after Chord.
