Arena → Style → Stonehenge is now a memory palace. It shows the monument as it stood, and every stone always has the same shape in the same place:

- the sarsen circle and its lintels
- the five trilithons, with the Great Trilithon at the back
- the bluestone circle and the bluestone horseshoe
- the Altar Stone
- the Slaughter and Heel Stones out along the avenue
- the four Station Stones on the bank, inside the Aubrey Holes

Click a stone to put a note on it. The stone glows in the note's level colour and carries its name. Click it again to open the note, or Option-click (Alt-click) to clear it. Hover a stone for its name; the stones keep Petrie's numbers where they're settled. Links between the notes you've placed run as faint paths on the grass.

The map frames the circle first. Zoom out to see the Heel, Slaughter and Station Stones.

0.8.81's version, where each note shaped its own stone, stays for now as Standing stones.
