## What's new in 0.8.63

- **Kandinsky → Composition,** a second style in Kandinsky's gear, after *Composition VIII* (1923). It paints one map and its links:
  - **The map:** the great dark circle with its halo, at the upper left.
  - **Its links:** shapes that fan out across warm paper. Hubs and maps of maps are haloed circles, maps are discs and rings, linked notes are triangles, half-circles, arcs and checkerboards, and the rest are dots. Each shape is bigger for a higher score.
  - **The lines:** a black line runs from the map to each link, curving into an arc for an up or related link. Thin coloured lines join the shapes that link to each other.
  - **Changing the painting:** pick any note to paint it with its own links instead.
