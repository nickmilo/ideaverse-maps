## What's new in 0.8.0

Sixty small releases since 0.7.0, gathered here. The headline is saved views, and how much they can do now. Close behind: emojis.

### Saved views (the 🔭 telescope, or V)
- **A view remembers the whole map.** The map, its settings and every filter: years, Organize by most recently modified and its window, folders, collections, MOCs, properties, bases. Also Show emojis, Base Builder's layout (Rows, Scatter, Mosaic, Constellation or Timeline) and what it reads, Linear Calendar's years and halves, the zoom and the selected note. Open a view and everything comes back.
- **Your own groups.** **+ Group** makes one (an Annual Review set, your Daily Drivers). Groups fold open and shut. Drag a view onto a group to move it in, drag between views to reorder, and drag a group to reorder groups. Double-click a group to rename it.
- **Rename, Update, delete.** Point at a view for Rename, Update (save what's on screen now) and ×.
- **On a phone,** rows show just the names. Tap **Edit** for each view's controls.
- **A file you own.** Views live in a plain JSON file in a folder you pick (the pill at the bottom of Saved), alongside your folder emojis.
- **New users start with an example:** a group called Base, with Emergence and its Space-Time version.
- **Tours** have their own button.

### Emojis
- **Show emojis** puts each note's emoji in place of its dot, bigger the higher its level. It's in the gear of Linear Calendar, Emergence and Space-Time, Sensemakers, Gravity, Starfield, Obsidian, Ideaverse, Chord and Tesla, Spiral, Lifeline and Tree. Each map remembers its own switch.
- **Display → Assign emojis.** Pick an emoji for a folder from a small grid, or paste any emoji, and every note in it gets one. A subfolder's emoji wins over its folder's.
- **Or bring them from a base.** A column of one of your bases (Settings → Emoji labels) fills in any note your folders don't cover. **Copy emojis from a base column** turns that column into folder emojis in one click.

### One maps button, one gear
- **☰ and the map's name** open every map, each with a small icon. The menu stays open as you hop between maps.
- **A number key for every map:** 1–9, then 0 for Linear Calendar, - = [ for the rest, and \ for Base Builder.
- **The gear** next to it holds this map's own settings, in one look across maps (Group's choices moved in here).
- **Show more maps in the header** puts eight more map names beside the button when there's room.

### Keys
- **Letters for the things you reach for:** O Orbit, J K L its speeds, A Animations, W Wander Mode, T Timelapse, S Sync, F Filter, N Focus, V Saved views.
- **?** opens a card of every shortcut, and hover hints show their key.

### Filter
- **MOCs:** keep one or several maps and the notes linked to them, 1 or 2 steps out.
- **Properties:** keep notes by any value of any shared property.
- **Organize by most recently modified,** with Last Day, Week, Month, Year or All, counted back from right now. At most 30 changed notes a day, with a burst of saves counted once.
- **A dot on each list** that's narrowing the map.

### More
- **Arrange by Link score:** the score that sets each note's level, around Gravity's circle or across any map with Arrange.
- **The toolbar** has evenly sized icons. On a narrow pane or phone it wraps one icon at a time.
- **Fit all** fills the whole map again on every map.
- **Bookshelf:** Show bookshelf auto-index system is a switch in its gear.
- **Quieter menus:** no hover pop-ups inside them.
- **Fixes:** the Years slider spans all your notes even after Organize by most recently modified; Years labels sit under their handles.
