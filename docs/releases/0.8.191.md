Filter → Years: **Last Day, Week, Month, Year or All** now works by date created too. The Last row is always there:

- With **Organize by most recently modified** off, it's the notes created in the last day, week, month or year, by their Date property. Emergence and Space-Time stretch that window across the timeline, with a day marked at a time.
- With it on, it works as before, by when each file last changed.

Each switch keeps its own choice, and the years slider steps aside while a window is picked. Saved views remember both.
