Cockpit arrivals no longer jump. The view used to hold still while the ship swung round the note, so the note slid off screen and then snapped back. Now the view stays on the note through the whole swing. The ship carries its speed in, curves round and eases out, and the swing starts gently. A flown arrival has no warp flash: the hyperspace streaks fade out and the new link names fade in.

Capture now records the Cockpit. Clips and stills of it used to come out blank.
