# 0.9.56

- **Pause the map behind the Holodeck** (Display → Editors). On: in the Holodeck the map behind your notes holds still. It stops redrawing and stops following the note you're in, and the veil over it loses its blur. The Links HUD still follows your notes. Turn it off, or leave the Holodeck, and the map catches up with the note you're in at once.
