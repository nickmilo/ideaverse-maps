# 0.9.156: Emergence · Book Cover

A new choice under Emergence, **Book Cover**, draws the map like the Linking Your Thinking cover:

- **Levels 3 to 5** are hollow, slightly oval rings, joined only by their strongest links. Each note links to its nearest linked neighbour, and a two-way link counts double, so they form chains, as on the cover.
- **Levels 1 and 2** are a field of small flecks below.
- **Colour** comes from where a note sits: a clock face of twelve pastels around the map's centre, the cover's own palette.

Hover or pick a note to see all its links.
