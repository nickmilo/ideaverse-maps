## What's new in 0.8.25

- **Mosaic → Ranked.** A third pattern for Mosaic, beside Emergence and Scattered: every note in rank order. Read it from the bottom left, left to right and row by row, up to #1 at the top right. A row is as tall as its notes are big, so the hubs at the top get the tallest rows. Marks on the right show where the rows reach #10, #100, #1,000 and so on. Pick it under Mosaic's options.
