## What's new in 0.8.10

- **Focus zoom: Normal or Close-Up.** In Starfield, Focus frames every focused note again by default (Normal). A small square button in the bottom right of the map, or the **Z** key, switches to **Close-Up**: the note centred, with its galaxy's hub just inside the edge. The map remembers your choice.
- **Star Chart in 3D.** Star Chart is now a switch that works over 2D or 3D, so the rings, signposts and its extras turn with the 3D galaxies. Starfield's menu reads 2D | 3D, then the Star Chart switch and its four extras.
