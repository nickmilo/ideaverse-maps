Cockpit in Starfield: arriving at a note now settles on a view of it, the way Starfield shows a picked note.

- **The camera:** after the jump the ship eases back and turns to face the note. It stops on the far side from its nearer links, far enough that note and links fit in the view together, with nothing left behind you.
- **The note:** the sky darkens round it, and its name is in yellow. A ring round it is split into arcs for its links above, below, up and related.
- **Its links:** gentle curves in Starfield's link colours run to named stars, replacing the panels. Click a name to lock on. Names never sit on each other or on the note's own name.
- **HUD:** the attitude ring is left out in Starfield, since the note's own ring now sits where it would be.
