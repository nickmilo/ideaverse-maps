One way to open notes, everywhere, as in Obsidian:

- **Click** picks a note; in Edit mode it opens in the left editor.
- **⌘-click** (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) opens it in a new tab: an orb when orbs are on, otherwise an Obsidian tab. It was the support note before.
- **⌘⌥-click** (Ctrl+Alt) opens it as the support note on the right; without Edit mode, in Obsidian's split to the right.
- **Shift-click** is left free. In the editors, Shift-click on a link extends the selection again, as Obsidian does.

This holds on the map, in the HUD links, the Cockpit (stars, names, scope dots, panels), previews, Linear Calendar's year panel and links inside the editors.

**⌥ means link, everywhere:**

- **⌥-drag** works in Arena, Stonehenge and Kandinsky too, and in the Cockpit (into an editor).
- **Arena and Stonehenge:** hover a filled slot and a small × appears; click it to empty the slot. This was ⌥-click.
- **Posters lanes:** hover a chip and click **only** to show just that one. This was ⌥/⌘-click.

**Where a link dropped on a note goes, to try out:** Display → Edit mode → ⌥-drop: **Related**, **Top** (the first line after the properties), **• Top** (the same, as a bullet) or **End** (the last line). Control as you drop still sends it to up.
