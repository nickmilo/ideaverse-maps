## What's new in 0.7.38

Fixes for the community directory's automated checks:

- The plugin's short description is now the first sentence of the README: "Visualize your notes across a suite of insanely cool interactive maps—Emergence, Ideaverse, Starfield, and many more." It's under 250 characters and doesn't use the word "Obsidian".
- The README's Privacy section now says what the plugin can reach:
  - It lists and reads your vault's notes.
  - Capture writes its pictures and clips to your attachments folder.
  - The Post on X and Instagram buttons open x.com and www.instagram.com, and only when you press them.
  - The Copy buttons write to the clipboard and never read it.
- Styles: removed the CSS the checker flags (`all: unset` and a column gap in a grid). Nothing looks different.
