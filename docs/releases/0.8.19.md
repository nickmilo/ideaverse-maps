## What's new in 0.8.19

- **Starfield's galaxy names sit at the back.** They're drawn with the sky, behind links, stars, note names and Star Chart's rings, so they never cover the notes you're reading. Only the Linking Your Thinking mark sits behind them. While a note is picked they fade further (to 38%). A click on a galaxy's name still flies you into it.
- **The picked note's name is always on top.** It's drawn last, after every other name, so a linked note's name can't cover it.
