## What's new in 0.8.31

- **Resize the side editors.** Drag the inner edge of the left editor or the support note to make it wider or narrower. Double-click the edge to go back to the default width.
- **Tuck an editor away from its header.** Click anywhere on the header of the left editor or the support note, its name included, to tuck it into its edge tab, the way the legend works. The buttons keep their own jobs.
