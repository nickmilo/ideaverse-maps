# 0.9.42

- **In the Holodeck, every note beside you has a tab.** A note that lands in the support note, from search, a link or the map, gets a tab right after the current one.
- **Swap the two notes by their title bars.** Drag the support note's title bar onto the primary note (or the primary's onto the support note) and they trade places. Like a tab dropped on the primary, it goes past a pin, and the primary stays pinned. A plain click on a title bar still tucks it away.
