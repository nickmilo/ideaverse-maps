# 0.9.158: Book Cover grows from the bottom up

On Emergence · Book Cover, a note's look follows its rank, not its level, so emergence reads as one gradual climb with no step anywhere:

- **Size:** marks grow steadily from the bottom up.
- **Shape:** solid flecks at the bottom, more and more open rings higher up, all rings at the top.
- **Ink:** gets stronger toward the top.
- **Links:** the web forms among the top few percent of notes and fades as it reaches down.
