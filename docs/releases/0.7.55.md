## What's new in 0.7.55

- **Saved on a phone shows the full view names.** Phones can't drag and drop, so the reorder and group controls used to crowd every row and cut the names short. Now each row shows just the view's name and its map. Tap **Edit**, next to + Group, to bring up each view's controls (↑ ↓, group, Update, ×) on a line of their own beneath its name, and tap **Done** to hide them again. On a computer nothing changes: dragging, with Update and × when you point at a view.
