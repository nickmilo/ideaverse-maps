HUD links on every map: Display → HUD links (off by default), with a Relevance scores switch beneath it (on). This is the Cockpit's list of the picked note's links, down the right of any map:

- each linked note once, marked ← links in, → links out or ↔ both
- up links first, then by relevance (100 for the most relevant)
- it curves less for busy notes so more fit, with "+N more" for the rest

Hover a name and it lights up with a line to its note on the map. Click it to go there. The list keeps clear of the note panel and the support note, and it's left out of the Cockpit (which has its own) and on phones.
