## What's new in 0.7.36

- **Emojis on Linear Calendar.** Turn on **Show emojis** under Linear Calendar's gear, and each note gets an emoji just left of its dot. The dots stay where they are.
- The emojis come from a column of one of your bases, computed the way Obsidian computes it, so a formula that picks an emoji by folder works as is. By default it's the first column of a base called Recents. Pick another base or column in **Settings → Emoji labels**.
- The emojis show once a day's box is big enough to hold them, and grow with the zoom.
