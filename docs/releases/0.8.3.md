## What's new in 0.8.3

- **Star Chart gets a second layer set, behind one mini switch.** In Starfield's gear, under Star Chart, turn on **Lines, readouts, gas and orbits** to add:
  - **Lines between hubs.** Every link between two maps or hubs (Level 4 and 5) is always drawn, brighter where both link each other: the backbone of your ideaverse. They step back while a note is picked.
  - **Readouts** beside each ringed hub: its level and score, links in and out, the year its linked notes began, and how many arrived this year.
  - **Gas.** Each galaxy glows in the color of the folder most of its notes live in, so Atlas, Calendar and Efforts show as regions of space.
  - **Orbits.** A dashed orbit turns around each galaxy, with a small moon riding it. Galaxies that gained more notes this past year turn faster.
- Turn the switch off for Star Chart as it was. Saved views remember the switch.
