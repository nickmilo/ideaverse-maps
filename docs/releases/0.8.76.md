## What's new in 0.8.76

- **Kandinsky is the Composition now.** The Squares style is gone. Kandinsky paints the note you pick and its links, after *Composition VIII*.
- **Every link, by default.** The composition paints every note the centre links to or from, instead of a number you chose. One switch in the gear, **Busy notes: show only their 24 strongest links**, keeps a heavily linked note readable.
- The gear is down to that switch and **Show note names**. A saved Squares choice opens as the Composition.
