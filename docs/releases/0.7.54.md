## What's new in 0.7.54

- **Fixed: switching saved views now switches every filter too.** A view used to bring back its map, years, folders and collections, but nothing newer. It now saves and restores:
  - Show notes outside the date range
  - Organize by most recently modified, with its Last Day · Week · Month · Year · All window
  - MOCs, with 1 or 2 steps
  - Properties
  - Bases
  - Linear Calendar's years and halves
- So a "this year" view and a "last week, by modified" view can sit side by side, and each opens exactly as saved.
- Views saved before 0.7.54 open with none of these filters and dated by the Date property. If one of them should use modified dates or a MOC, set it up and press Update on its row once.
