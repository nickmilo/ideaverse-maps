# 0.9.142

- **A map's look for Place by coordinates (Star Chart).** When Starfield is placed by coordinates, the Chart now paints:
  - each region as a soft colored band, warm white and gold in the middle out to blue at the rim, where its notes are thickest;
  - the routes: a note without coordinates that links to three or more placed notes is drawn as a line through them, the six longest bright, the rest faint, smoothed along their course;
  - a faint spiral of dust behind it all.
  Region names come from the region notes themselves. Vaults without coordinates see no change.
