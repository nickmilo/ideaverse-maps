Linear Calendar: a year layer for annual reviews (Nick: make it "less difficult to recollect what happened, and much easier to zoom out, identify patterns and discover what themes of the year start to emerge"). It is built from three notes: the year note (titled "2026"), the Preview note in the same folder, and the reviews beside them.

- **Year theme:** the theme from the Preview note sits beside the year: a `theme:` property, or else the first bold line under its Theme heading (🎻 Year of Making Inroads).
- **Recall** (in the space left of the months): 📅 Daily notes, 📚 Books noted, 🎬 Movies noted, 📺 Shows noted and ⭐ Marked for the year (notes that point up to the year note). Hover a count and its days light up; click it to keep them lit. Books, movies and shows are the notes with YearXP (or yearXPL) of that year in the Books, Movies or TV collections, placed on the day each note was made.
- **Reflect** (right of the months): the theme (click it to open the Preview note), the reviews in date order, and ▸ Extra, the rest of the year's review folder, folded until you open it. Hover a note to see its day; click it to open it.
- **Glow:** notes that point up to the year note glow gold.

Three switches in the Linear Calendar menu: Year theme, Year panel and Glow marked notes, all on by default. The panels need room beside the months (Half months on a wide pane), and step aside on a phone.
