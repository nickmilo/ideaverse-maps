## What's new in 0.7.58

- **Rename a saved view.** Point at a view in Saved and click **Rename**; its name turns into a box. Enter keeps it, Escape cancels. On a phone, tap **Edit** first.
- **Saved views remember more.** A view now keeps Show emojis (Linear Calendar, Emergence, Sensemakers) and Base Builder's layout: Rows, Scatter, Mosaic, Constellation or Timeline, along with what each axis, row and size reads and your slider ranges. A Mosaic view comes back as Mosaic. Views saved before this need an **Update** to pick these up.
- **The Years slider spans all your notes again.** If the map opened with Organize by most recently modified on, the slider took its years from when files were last changed (2024–2026, say) and kept them after you turned it off. It now always reads the Date property.
- **Sensemakers can show emojis.** A Show emojis switch in its gear, like Linear Calendar's and Emergence's.
- **Arrange by Link score.** Gravity, and the other maps with Arrange, can place notes by the score that sets each note's level.
- **No more hover labels on menus.** Saved ("Saved views and tours") and the gear menu showed a label under them when you pointed inside.
