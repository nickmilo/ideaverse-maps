Cockpit: True or false, a mini game. Turn it on with its switch in the Cockpit's ⚙.

A card at the top of the view makes a statement about the note you're on and another note. For example:

- "Innovations create unexpected second-order effects" links to "Pace Layers"
- "Stewart Brand" is a higher level than "Brian Eno"
- one note was written before the other
- two notes live in the same folder

Press 1 for true or 2 for false (or click the buttons).

- **Right:** the card glows green and you make a hyperspace jump to that note, where the next question waits.
- **Wrong:** the ship shudders, the card turns red, and it tells you the truth.

Your streak and your best are kept. It works in both worlds, but Starfield makes the jumps worth it.
