## What's new in 0.8.41

- **Arena, a new map:** a ring of slots you fill with notes, a workspace of your own.
  - **Filling it:** click an empty slot (+) and choose a note from the search box. Option-click (Alt-click on Windows and Linux) a slot to empty it.
  - **Links:** the links between the ring's notes cross the middle as curves, in their link colors, the way Chord draws them.
  - **The centre:** the note pinned in the editor sits there. With nothing pinned, the note you picked does, if it isn't already on the ring.
  - **The gear:** set how many slots (8 to 20), fill the empty slots from your tabs (the orbs), or empty the ring.
  - **Saved:** the ring is kept between sessions. In Edit mode, Arena frames itself between the editors.
- Arena and Compass no longer swing the camera off to where a searched note sat on the last map. They frame themselves around it instead.
