# 0.9.110

- **Filter the Links pane by folder.** A funnel button between Sort and Display opens the folders this note's links live in, as a tree with counts. Click a folder to hide its notes and everything under it, for example AIOS → History to drop the Daily Briefs. Click it again to bring them back, or use Show all. The funnel lights while anything is hidden, and every Links pane remembers the choice.
