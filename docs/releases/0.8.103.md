Wander Mode comes to the Cockpit. Press ▶ under Animations → Wander Mode, press P, or use the new Wander row in the Cockpit's ⚙. There are three ways to wander:

- **Links:** the ship turns toward one of the note's links, flies there on autopilot, lingers, then picks the next link. It favours the most relevant and backtracks at dead ends.
- **Jumps:** the ship turns toward a note anywhere in your vault, then makes a hyperspace jump straight to it.
- **Drift:** the ship cruises slowly while the lock passes from link to link as it looks at each, then flies to the last one.

The message line says where you're headed. Any key, drag or scroll hands the stick back to you. Wander Mode's seconds slider sets how long the ship lingers.
