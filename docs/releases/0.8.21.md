## What's new in 0.8.21

- **Edit mode's card looks like Obsidian.** The map's own colors no longer leak into the editor (cyan text, stray accent lines). The card follows your Obsidian theme by default.
- **Editor settings, under Display → Edit mode (experimental):**
  - **Editor theme: Obsidian or Map.** Map gives the card Obsidian's dark or light colors to match the map you're on, so a dark map like LYT Mode gets a dark editor.
  - **Opacity**, 50–100%, 84% to start: the map shows faintly through the card.
- **The map's menus open over the card**, not under it.
