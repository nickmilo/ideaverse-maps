Cockpit ⚙ → Previews: Off, One, Fewer, Natural or Max, as Compass has.

- **One:** what you had before: the note you're hovering, or else the locked note.
- **Fewer, Natural and Max:** also preview the most relevant links in view, up to 5, 10 or 18. They get smaller the more there are, at Compass's sizes.

Each preview sits beside its star where there's room, never on another preview, the names down the sides, the instruments or the cockpit note. They hide while you fly on autopilot or Wander. This replaces the earlier "Preview the locked note" switch; if you had it off, Previews starts at Off.
