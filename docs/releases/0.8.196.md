Closing and pinning the main editor's note, the Obsidian way (Nick):

- **⌘W** (Obsidian's Close tab, on whatever hotkey you've given it) closes the note in the left editor and its orb, not the map. The next orb's note takes its place, as when you close a tab in Obsidian. With no note in the editor, ⌘W closes the map as before.
- **The editor's ×** does the same: the note and its orb close together.
- **Your Toggle pin hotkey** (Obsidian's own, ⌘⇧E for you) pins the left editor's note, and unpins it.

Both answer only while the map or its editor has the focus.
