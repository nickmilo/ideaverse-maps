## What's new in 0.7.37

- **Recent Breadcrumbs**, a new map under ☰ → Experimental (at the top), is a trail of the notes you changed lately:
  - **Newest at the top left.** Three notes to a column, each a little further in, then the next column.
  - **One section per day.** Each day starts a new column under its name (Today, Yesterday, Fri 23, Oct 12…), with "+N" when the day let notes go.
  - **Your emojis** appear before the names, from the same base column as Linear Calendar (Settings → Emoji labels).
  - **At most 30 notes a day.** A burst of changes from one edit counts once, by its biggest note.
- **It's made for a short, wide pane.** Split one at the bottom of your workspace and switch it to Recent Breadcrumbs.
  - The text is a touch smaller than your note text (seven-eighths of it, so it follows your font size) and stays that size.
  - The wheel scrolls back in time; ⌘ or Ctrl with the wheel zooms.
  - Clicking a crumb picks the note, as on any map.
