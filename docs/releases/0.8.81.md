Arena gets a third style, Stonehenge (⚙ → Style, still experimental). It shows your slots as a stone circle, seen from above and in front.

- A stone's height is its level, and its width is its link score.
- Sarsens (warm grey) are maps and hubs. Bluestones (blue-grey) are the rest.
- Two neighbouring stones that link carry a lintel across their tops.
- Links across the circle are worn paths on the grass.
- The centre note lies flat in the middle as the altar stone.
- Click an empty footprint (+) to raise a stone there.

The Board style drops "(experimental)" from its name, since the whole of Arena now sits under Experimental → Unfinished.
