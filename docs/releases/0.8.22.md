## What's new in 0.8.22

Edit mode (experimental) grows to three editors and hub previews. All of them are under **Display → Edit mode (experimental)**.

- **Opacity reaches the whole editor.** The editor no longer paints its own background, so the map shows through all of it, not just the header.
- **Open the editor on the left side.** A switch that puts the main editor in a tall panel on the left instead of the card under the note. The note you're on moves to the middle of the space that's left.
- **A second editor on the right, Roam-style.** In Edit mode, ⌘-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) a note on the map to open it in an editor on the right, beside the note panel, while the main editor keeps its note. The centre card moves over to fit between them.
- **Hub previews (Starfield).** A switch that shows a small, read-only preview above each big galaxy's hub note: its title and the start of its text. They appear as you zoom in, up to ten at a time.
- **One theme and one opacity for all of them**: the centre card, the left and right editors, and the previews.
