## What's new in 0.7.57

- **Display → Assign emojis.** Give each folder an emoji, and every note in it shows that emoji in place of its dot on Linear Calendar and Emergence (turn on **Show emojis** in each map's gear). The folders are listed like Filter → Folders. Click the gray smiley beside a folder to pick from a small grid, or paste any emoji. A subfolder's emoji wins over its folder's. Your choices are kept with your saved views, in the views file in your vault.
- **Copy emojis from a base column.** If your emojis already come from a base (Settings → Emoji labels), one click turns them into folder emojis: each folder takes the emoji most of its notes have there. A note whose folders have no emoji still takes its emoji from the base.
