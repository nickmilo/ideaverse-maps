## What's new in 0.8.15

- **The note you're on stands out.** Its name is larger and in the accent color, and the names of its linked notes step back to 70%, so it's easy to tell which one is picked, on every map.
- **Close-Up zoom on more maps.** The square zoom button and **Z** now work on Emergence, Space-Time, Gravity, Obsidian, Sensemakers, Chord, Linear Calendar, Mosaic, Treemap, Bookshelf, Tree, Spiral, Lifeline and Geometric, as well as Starfield. Close-Up centres the note you're on and zooms so most of its linked notes sit just inside the view (in Starfield: the note with its galaxy's hub). Normal shows the whole map again. Maps you turn in 3D keep their own camera.
