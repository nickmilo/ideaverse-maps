# 0.9.57

- **Dragging an Obsidian tab into the Holodeck is steady now.** While a tab was dragged over the map, Obsidian offered to split its layout there and laid its preview over the whole window, covering the Holodeck's tabs and making the drop flicker. Now, over the Holodeck (or, outside it, over the map's tabs and notes), Obsidian doesn't offer that split and its preview clears, so the drop line shows where the tab will land. Over Obsidian's own panes, its split preview works as before. Files from the sidebar, bases, search and linked mentions were already steady.
