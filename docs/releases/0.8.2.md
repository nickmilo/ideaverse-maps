## What's new in 0.8.2

- **Star Chart, a third way to see Starfield.** In Starfield's gear, pick **Star Chart** next to 2D and 3D. It keeps the 2D galaxies and lays a navigator's chart over them:
  - **Rings around the hub you're on.** The inner ring shows its links by kind, in the link colors: up, related, above, below. The middle ring is a dial of time, from your first year at twelve o'clock round to today, with a tick for each month its linked notes were made. The outer ring holds **signposts**.
  - **Signposts point the way.** Each one sits on the ring in the direction of a map or hub the note links to. Faraway ones carry the hub's name. Click a signpost to go there; it gets its own rings, so you can hop hub to hub across the ideaverse.
  - With nothing picked, the four biggest hubs wear full rings, and every other Level 5 hub wears a faint broken arc.
  - Saved views remember Star Chart.
