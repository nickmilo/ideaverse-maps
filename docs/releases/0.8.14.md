## What's new in 0.8.14

- **Zoom: Normal or Close-Up, anywhere in Starfield.** The toggle is now a fourth zoom button, under +, − and fit: a square with a dot that grows when Close-Up is on. It works with any note picked, not only in Focus: Close-Up frames the note with its galaxy's hub, Normal shows the whole sky again. **Z** does the same.
- **The zoom buttons stay beside the note panel** instead of hiding under it.
- **Star Chart's readouts are easier to see.** Each one sits on a small dark card, drawn above the names, and moves around its ring (upper right first) to the spot with the fewest names and notes in the way, never on top of another readout.
- **Starfield: the Linking Your Thinking mark sits at a quarter of the width**, clear of the galaxies below the middle.
- **Scoring explains the levels in plain words:** a note gets the highest level it qualifies for, with what each level takes.
