# 0.9.153: Base Builder takes the Years filter

Every Base Builder layout now uses the **Years** section in Filters: a range of years, the Recent windows, fixed dates, or by modified. It decides which notes count. Each layout's own slider still narrows the property it's built on, such as a release year or a rating.

With **Show notes outside the date range** on (the default), notes outside the dates, or with no date, stay on the map. They're faded, in a row, block or column of their own called "Outside the dates". In Tally they pile up in that column without stretching the calendar. Turn the switch off and they leave.
