# 0.8.247

- Recents takes a new shape: narrow on the left and opening out to the right, its left edge on the rows' arc and shorter, the top rising and the bottom falling to a full-height right edge, with small brackets at the corners. The panel is only as wide as its names, and its left edge always clears them.
- More Recents (the Show slider) makes the panel taller, never the rows tighter: the arc opens up as the list grows, and the panel fits the map's height before it scrolls.
