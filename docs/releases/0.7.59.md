## What's new in 0.7.59

- **Rename is small again.** In Saved, the Rename button had stretched across the row and covered the view's name. It's now a quiet word beside Update and ×, like them.
- **Every saved view shows its map's name.** Views of Base Builder read "Base Builder" (it said "builder"), and the same goes for Posters, Obsidian, Mosaic, People and Ideaverse.
