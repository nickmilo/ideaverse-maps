# 0.9.139

- **Place by coordinates (Starfield).** A vault whose notes carry two number properties for a position (Settings → Coordinate properties; `x` and `y` by default) can lay Starfield out by them instead of by links: a map's grid, a galaxy's, a city's. Turn it on in Star Chart's gear (Place by coordinates, shown once at least 20 notes have both). Notes without coordinates sit beside the notes they link to; the Ship flies the same layout. Off by default; vaults without coordinates see no change.
