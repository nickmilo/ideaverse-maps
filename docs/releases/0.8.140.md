Cockpit on a phone:

- **Upright:** the overview map moves to the top right, the scopes sit in the bottom corners and no longer overlap, and the note card steps aside. The link names are smaller and tighter.
- **On its side:** the plugin's top bar and trail step aside so the map gets the height, and they come back when you turn the phone upright. In a short view the link list shows fewer names, 12 px apart, instead of piling onto one line. Previews fit the height.

Obsidian's own title bar and bottom bar still take their share of a landscape screen; the plugin can't hide those.
