# 0.8.220

- An experiment: Starfield as one place with two vantage points. A small **Chart | Starship** switch at the top of Starfield (and in its gear, in place of 2D/3D) boards the Starship at the note you're on, among the same galaxies; **Chart** (or **C**) brings you back to the star you were at. 3D Starfield steps aside for the trial.
- Starship on its own (from the maps menu) is unchanged: the trip borrows its Starfield world without saving it, and hands your own setting back.
