## What's new in 0.7.30

- **Press ? for a card of every keyboard shortcut**: the map keys, in the maps list's current order, and the keys for things on the map. It also opens from the last row of Display (Keyboard shortcuts). Escape, ? again, or a click elsewhere closes it.
- **Hover hints show their key.** For example, the Orbit play button reads "Drift (O)", and Filter reads "Filter (F)". The same goes for Wander Mode, Timelapse, Sync, Focus and the Animations tab, and the key stays when a hint changes from Play to Stop.
